Experimental Assessment Prompts for Students

Use these structured experiments in class so students test cause-and-effect rather than guessing:
  • Experiment 1 — +V Rail Isolation: With light above threshold and valid battery wires, toggle the +V Rail SPST between CLOSED and OPEN. Record where charge stops and why neither output works when open.
  • Experiment 2 — Resistor Impact (R1 & R2): Increase R1 (LED series resistor) from 100 Ω to 1 kΩ and observe LED brightness; then change R2 (LDR bias resistor) and observe how the effective trigger point shifts.
  • Experiment 3 — Accessibility & Flash Rate: Compare slow flashing (1 Hz) vs. faster flashing (4 Hz) alongside the buzzer—which rate is easiest to notice without being harsh?
  • Experiment 4 — Driver Stage & Power Limits: Switch between Direct and Transistor Driver across 4.5V, 6V, and 9V battery packs (keeping power at or below 9V) and note when outputs remain steady vs. drop out.
