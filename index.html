Experimental Assessment Tasks for Students

  • Experiment 1 (Transistor Terminals A, B, C): Trace how signal voltage enters (B) Base from the LDR/10kΩ divider, switching current from (C) Collector out through (A) Emitter to the return rail.
  • Experiment 2 (Switch Control on Supply): With the alarm active, open the SPST Switch and verify that both the flashing LED and buzzer turn OFF immediately.
  • Experiment 3 (Series Resistor R1 Effect): Change the LED series resistor between 100 Ω, 330 Ω, and 1 kΩ while keeping light above threshold; explain how R1 affects LED brightness.
  • Experiment 4 (Battery Wiring & Polarity Faults): Disconnect or reverse a battery lead and record which symptoms appear in the status bar and charge flow.
